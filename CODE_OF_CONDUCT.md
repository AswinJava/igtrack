# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person. This project
is about telling the truth clearly; do that in issues and pull requests too.

## Expected behaviour

- **Critique work, not people.** "This fabricates a claim from a partial scan"
  is useful. "You are careless" is not.
- **Be honest about what you verified.** Say what you ran and what you did not.
  "I could not reproduce this on Windows" is a contribution.
- **Respect the project's red lines.** Requests to add access-control
  circumvention, scraping of private data, or evasion of platform protections
  will be refused. That refusal is the project working as intended.
- **No harassment.** Not about age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
  identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
  race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- **No spam or self-promotion.** Promotion of paid monitoring or scraping
  services is off-topic here.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
space where you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts for
behaviour that violates this document. Enforcement is at maintainer discretion
and need not be explained in detail, though it usually will be.

If you experience or witness a violation, contact the maintainer privately
(a private advisory on the repository is a reliable channel).
